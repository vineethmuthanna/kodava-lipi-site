Kodava Lipi

A dedicated script for the Kodava language

What is Kodava Lipi?

Kodava Lipi is a newly designed writing system created specifically for the Kodava language. It represents Kodava sounds clearly and consistently, without relying on adaptations of other scripts.

Why a new script?

Kodava has unique sounds and pronunciation patterns that are not fully represented by existing scripts. Kodava Lipi addresses this by providing a one-to-one mapping between sound and symbol.
